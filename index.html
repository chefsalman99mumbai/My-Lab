The Hotel Bostan story

Good food.
Good company.
No hurry.

Hotel Bostan is built around an uncomplicated idea: food tastes better when there is time around it. Our kitchen follows the rhythm of the day — coffee in the morning, generous plates at lunch, intimate dinners and something sweet whenever the evening asks for it.
